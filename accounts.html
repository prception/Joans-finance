Accounts

Bank accounts, deposits, trading accounts and crypto wallets — where the money sits.

Add Account

Fixed and financial-institution deposits are recorded here, as accounts (with interest rate and maturity), not as investments, so they're never counted twice. Holdings bought through a trading account or wallet are investments; only the uninvested cash belongs here.

Transfer Between Accounts

Moving money between your own accounts is a transfer — it changes balances but is never counted as income or expense.
